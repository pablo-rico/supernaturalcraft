# SupernaturalCraft

**Hunt demons. Draw the circle. Open the Cage.**

SupernaturalCraft is a NeoForge mod inspired by the TV series *Supernatural*. Start as a hunter with a pocket of salt and a
lighter, learn the trade from your Hunter's Journal, and fight your way from yellow-eyed demons all the way up to the Cage, the
Horsemen, the archangels and the Author himself.

> Unofficial fan project. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Warner Bros., The CW or the creators of *Supernatural*. All art,
> models, sounds and code are original.

## Features

### The hunt
- **Hunter's gear**: salt lines demons can't cross, chalk, holy water, devil's traps, Ruby's knife, angel blades and the Colt.
- **Creatures**: black-eyed demons, occultists, hellhounds, ghosts, vampires, werewolves, shapeshifters, witches and more, each
  with its own weakness.
- **Crossroads deals**: make a deal with a crossroads demon... and pay when the hounds come.
- **Weapons that grow with you**: weapon profiles, runes, curses, the Infernal Forge and ascension.

### Magic
- **Enochian sigils**: compose your own spells from forms, effects and modifiers, across four schools (Enochian, Infernal,
  Witchcraft, Hunter) with mastery ranks.
- **Rituals**: draw ritual circles around an altar, place the offerings, and summon, bind, consecrate or forge.
- **Spell bowl witchcraft**: hex bags, taglocks and targeted curses, recited over a lit bowl.

### Bosses
**21 boss fights**, each with its own arena, phases, telegraphed attacks and cinematics. The main road runs through Azazel,
Lilith, Lucifer, the Four Horsemen, the Broken Chorus, Metatron, Amara, Lucifer Uncaged and Michael, up to Chuck. Side roads
lead to Gabriel, Raphael, Cain, Abaddon, Rowena or the Grand Witch, Naomi and Zachariah. Arenas restore the terrain when the
fight ends, and summoning items are given back if you lose.

### The world
- **Hell** and **Heaven** dimensions, including a personal Heaven for every hunter, with memories to relive.
- **Factions**: side with Heaven or Hell, rise through the ranks, and unlock powers on a power wheel (with winged flight).
- **The Men of Letters**: find the bunker, join the order, research, and work procedurally generated cases from the War Table.
- **The Mark of Cain**, the coven and the Book of the Damned: choices with consequences.
- **Structures**: the Hymnal Spire, graves, natural crossroads, the Author's cabin and more.

### The Hunter's Journal
An in-game book that unlocks as you play: a bestiary, lore, the spell scriptorium and a roadmap of the whole progression, so you
always know where to go next.

## Requirements

| | |
|---|---|
| Minecraft | 1.21.1 |
| Loader | NeoForge 21.1.221+ |
| [GeckoLib](https://modrinth.com/mod/geckolib) | 4.9+ (required, client and server) |
| PlayerAnimationLib | 1.1.6+ (required on clients) |
| [JEI](https://modrinth.com/mod/jei) | optional: ritual, sigil and spell bowl recipes |
| [Curios](https://modrinth.com/mod/curios) | optional: amulet slots |

Works in single player and on dedicated servers.

## Status

The mod is in active development (0.x): expect balance changes, and back up your worlds before updating. Bug reports and
feedback are very welcome.
